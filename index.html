The UCG Grading Scale

Three graders look at every card on its own. The grade on the label is the one they agree on.

10Gem Mint

Four sharp corners, full gloss, 55/45 centering or better on the front.

9Mint

One minor flaw, such as a slight wax stain or tiny print spot.

8NM-MT

Slight fraying on one or two corners. 65/35 centering or better.

7Near Mint

Light surface wear, minor corner fraying, gloss mostly intact.

6EX-MT

Visible surface wear or a light print line.

5Excellent

Minor corner rounding, some loss of gloss, light edge chipping.

4VG-EX

Rounded corners, noticeable wear, maybe a light crease.

3Very Good

Rounding on corners, gloss mostly gone, light crease visible.

2Good

Heavy corner rounding, obvious wear, several creases.

1Poor

Missing pieces, major creasing, or heavy discoloration.
